## Cherrygram for macOS

Cherrygram is a messaging client for macOS. It's a fork of Telegram for macOS, but with some added features.

## Features

* Message your friends
* Send photos and videos
* Share files
* Make voice and video calls
* Create and join groups
* Create and join channels
* Share your location
* Share your contacts
* Share your screen
